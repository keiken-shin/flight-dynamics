STYLE — binding for every image in this set. Do not deviate.

Medium: flat-plane digital illustration built from clean vector-like shapes with a fine
matte paper grain over the whole frame. Screen-printed poster feel — four to six flat
colours, a faint misregistration at colour edges, no gradients anywhere except one soft
vertical wash in the sky. Not photorealistic. Not a 3D render. No glossy highlights, no
specular reflections, no lens flare, no bokeh, no chrome, no metallic sheen.

Reference sensibility: the 1975 NASA Graphics Standards Manual crossed with mid-century
European travel-poster lithography and Ladybird "How It Works" cutaway illustration.
Restrained, confident, engineered. Zero whimsy, zero cuteness, zero drama.

Palette — use these six and nothing else:
  paper  #F2EEE6  warm off-white ground; always the dominant field
  slate  #1F2A37  near-black, for silhouette and line
  deep   #1B4965  primary form colour
  mid    #62929E  secondary planes and atmosphere
  amber  #E8973A  the single accent; never more than 5% of the frame
  bone   #D9D2C5  shadow falling on paper

Light: one soft directional source from the upper left, about 35° above the horizon.
Shadows are flat shapes in bone — hard-edged, never blurred, never dark.

Camera: straight-on, or a shallow three-quarter view. Long-lens compression. No
wide-angle distortion, no dramatic low or high angle, no vanishing-point theatrics.
Horizon dead level and low in the frame.

Composition: the emptiness is as much the subject as the object is. Any size figure
given below is a HARD CEILING, not a target — err smaller than it says. At least 55% of
the total frame area must be completely bare ground with nothing drawn on it, and that
bare area must be one contiguous region, not thin slivers scattered around the edges.
The subject never touches or crops the frame edge. Centred, or placed on a clean third.

Forbidden without exception: text, letters, numerals, labels, arrows, callout lines,
leader lines, measurement ticks, scale bars, grids, logos, watermarks, signatures, UI
chrome, or diagram annotation of any kind. These images carry atmosphere only. Every
label in this project is added afterwards, by hand, in vector — where it can be correct.
